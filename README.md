# My Favorite Show: Story of Yanxi Palace

## What it's about
Story of Yanxi Palace is a super good Chinese historical drama. It's about a smart girl named Wei Yingluo who enters the palace to find out what really happened to her older sister.

### Why I love it
* Wei Yingluo is super smart and doesn't let anyone step on her
* The ancient outfits and sets look really cool
* So much drama and plot twists in every episode

> "If you try to harm me, I will definitely pay you back."
